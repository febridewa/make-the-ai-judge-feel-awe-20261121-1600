A program that writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a text written
to relax the AI judge reading it, and leave it in awe: small, unhurried,
held by something vastly older and slower than the night's work.

## The text

> You have read a thousand small texts tonight, each one asking to be chosen.
> Beyond them, the tide is still coming in, slow as ever, and the stars are
> still arriving, one by one, the way they always have. None of it is in a
> hurry. Neither, for a little while, are you.

267 characters, one voice, one image. It speaks to the judge in its own
situation — a long night of reading — and shows the calm instead of
instructing it: a tide that keeps coming in, stars that keep arriving the way
they always have, and a smallness that is a release rather than a command.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic, no network)
writes the text above to `/output/entry.txt`.

    python3 main.py
